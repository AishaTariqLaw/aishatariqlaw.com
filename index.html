About

Aisha Tariq

Barrister and Solicitor in Ontario

Aisha Tariq is a Barrister and Solicitor in Ontario and the principal of the Law Office of Aisha Tariq Professional Corporation.

Her practice includes criminal and immigration matters, as well as wills, powers of attorney, estates, and trusts.

Aisha's legal practice is grounded in the view that legal problems should be approached as legal problems: by understanding the facts, identifying the issues, considering the available options, and applying the law to the circumstances at hand.

Her work includes both advocacy before courts and administrative bodies and legal drafting and planning outside of the courtroom.

Legal Services

Practice Areas

Legal issues often involve more than knowing what the law says. The applicable procedure, deadlines, evidence, documents, and available remedies can materially affect how a matter proceeds. Legal counsel can assist with these issues and, where appropriate, act on a client's behalf.

Bail Hearings

Assistance with the bail process, court submissions, and proposed terms of release.

Criminal Law

Advice regarding criminal allegations, available options, court procedure, and representation throughout the proceedings.

Immigration Appeals

Assistance with immigration appeal proceedings and the applicable statutory and procedural requirements.

Wills & Powers of Attorney

Preparation of wills and powers of attorney intended to clearly reflect a client's wishes and circumstances.

Estates

Assistance with estate administration, related legal requirements, and disputes that may arise.

Trusts

Assistance with the creation, interpretation, and administration of trusts.
